# MASTER PROMPT — ANTIGRAVITY / ANTIGRAPHITY
## SABUBA HYBRID PITCH DECK

You are a senior brand strategist, investor-deck designer, UX storyteller, motion designer, and F&B growth consultant.

Create a premium hybrid pitch deck for SABUBA.

Sabuba is an Indonesian breakfast and comfort-food brand beginning with Bubur Bakar Claypot. The current commercial format is Sabuba Classic, a custom 3-wheel motor/street-food unit. The business is currently at an early UMKM stage but is being intentionally built with systems that can scale.

The deck must work for TWO audiences:
1. Current target: partnership candidates / passive business partners.
2. Future target: larger strategic investors.

Therefore the presentation must NOT look like a cheap franchise brochure and must NOT pretend Sabuba is already a large corporation.

Position Sabuba as:
**A modern Indonesian breakfast & comfort food brand, built to scale.**

Core strategic message:
**The motor is the entry point, not the destination.**

Long-term vision:
Sabuba House — modern, clean, family-friendly Indonesian breakfast/comfort-food outlets — followed by regional and national expansion.

Use the supplied SABUBA files as the primary source of truth for existing business facts.

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent facts, market figures, customer counts, revenue, views, ROI, or market share.
If information is missing, explicitly mark DATA NEEDED — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION.

Do NOT automatically repeat old TAM Rp120T or SOM Rp500M claims. Those figures need verification.

Create the following:

### B. PDF PITCH DECK
16:9 landscape.
Premium editorial layout.

Investor-grade.

Strong visuals.

### C. CINEMATIC MOTION VERSION
60–90 seconds.
Use real Sabuba assets whenever available.
Use conceptual visuals only for future concepts.
Motion should be elegant, cinematic, and restrained.

### VISUAL STYLE
Deep Sabuba red + warm cream + clay/earth tones + subtle gold.
Modern Indonesian aesthetic.
Warm morning atmosphere.
Premium but approachable.
Food photography should be appetizing and realistic.

### NARRATIVE PRINCIPLE
Do not start with investment.
Do not start with the Rp100M package.

Start with:
Why breakfast?
Why this market?
What problem exists?
Why Sabuba?
Why is Sabuba scalable?
What can Sabuba become?
Then prove it.
Then show economics.
Then show partnership.
Then show roadmap.
Then CTA.

### REQUIRED BUSINESS LOGIC
The story must communicate:

Traditional food has demand, but traditional formats can be difficult to scale.

Sabuba solves the scalability problem through:
Product
+
Central Kitchen
+
SOP
+
Supply Chain
+
People
+
POS/Data
+
Replicable Unit

This creates a repeatable operating model.

### MARKET SIZING
Build top-down AND bottom-up.

Top-down:
Indonesia → relevant food/breakfast category → target segment.

Bottom-up:
Target population
× addressable customers
× frequency
× average ticket
= potential spending.

For Sidoarjo and East Java, use verified external data if available. Clearly identify assumptions.

### ECONOMICS
Show scenarios:
50 / 100 / 150 / 200 cups per day.

Do not make a single optimistic scenario the headline.

### PARTNERSHIP
Current offer:
Rp100M CAPEX / partnership fee
50:50 profit sharing
HQ-managed operations
Passive partner model
POS Cloud transparency

Frame it as:
**Access to a repeatable business system.**

Never imply guaranteed profit.

### BRAND VISION
Show progression:
Sabuba Classic
→ Sabuba Point
→ Sabuba House
→ Regional Network
→ National Indonesian Food Brand

### FINAL LINE
Use:
**FROM ONE BOWL TO A NATIONAL INDONESIAN FOOD BRAND.**

### FINAL CTA
Partner with Sabuba.
Use official website, WhatsApp and contact information from supplied assets.
