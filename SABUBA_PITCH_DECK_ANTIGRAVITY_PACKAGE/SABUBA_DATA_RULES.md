# SABUBA — DATA & CLAIM RULES

1. Never fabricate market size.
2. Never fabricate BPS figures.
3. Never fabricate outlet revenue.
4. Never present assumptions as facts.
5. Label assumptions clearly.
6. Cite the source and year for every external market figure.
7. Distinguish:
   - historical actual
   - management target
   - forecast
   - assumption
8. Do not claim guaranteed profit or guaranteed ROI.
9. Existing proposal figures must be treated as management/proposal figures until independently verified.
10. Proposal's current facts include:
   - CAPEX Rp100M
   - 50:50 profit sharing
   - HQ-managed operations
   - Sabuba Classic motor custom 3-wheel format
   - Central Kitchen
   - menu categories
   - selected TikTok social proof
11. Any missing information should be marked:
   DATA NEEDED — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION
